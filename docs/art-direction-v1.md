## Direction A — “Silent Frame”

**Feeling:** Museum dark. The product is the only light source. Apple-level quiet confidence.

**Inspiration (signature mechanics):**
1. Apple AirPods Pro — pinned scroll, copy that arrives after the object proves itself
2. BeoPlay H6 — sparse type, culture over feature dump
3. Apple product pages — huge tracking-tight headlines, vast black negative space

**Palette:** Pure locked tokens. Accent `#007AFF` used **only** on focus rings + one primary text link — never as a neon wash.

**Fonts:**
- Display: **Geist Sans** or **Satoshi** (tight `-0.03em` on H1)
- Body: **Inter** is banned → **Geist Sans** same family at regular, or **IBM Plex Sans** for body calm

**Signature moment:** Hero sequence freezes on last frame → wordmark **AURA** and one concrete H1 bleed in from a vertical mask (no fade-from-black on LCP poster). Floating “Order Now” appears only after 100vh.

**Why it wins conversion:** Justifies $349 by looking like the company already doesn’t need to shout.

---

## Direction B — “Titanium Instrument”

**Feeling:** Precision tool. Lab photography. Metal, glass, measured specs as poetry.

**Inspiration (signature mechanics):**
1. BeoPlay H6 — material honesty, lifestyle as proof
2. IN2 headphones — product as hero object in commerce
3. B&O / Leica-adjacent product sites — macro metal + short technical lines

**Palette:** `#000` field · cards `#1C1C1E` with multi-layer contact shadows · hairline `#86868B/40` rules · accent `#007AFF` as a thin “signal LED” on cards (2px left bar / focus), not big fills.

**Fonts:**
- Display: **Neue Haas Grotesk / Helvetica Now** substitute → **Neue Montreal** or **General Sans**
- Mono specs: **JetBrains Mono** or **Geist Mono** for “40 hr” / “ANC” numerals

**Signature moment:** Features grid as instrument panel — glass cards; on scroll-in, mono numerals count/settle (50, not “amazing battery”). Deep Dive 50/50 with macro placeholder marked Simulation.

**Why it wins conversion:** Speaks to audiophiles/creatives who distrust lifestyle fluff.

---

## Direction C — “Blue Signal”

**Feeling:** Dark OLED cinema with a controlled electric pulse — still premium, slightly more campaign energy.

**Inspiration (signature mechanics):**
1. Apple AirPods Pro — cinematic scroll object
2. Coloud Headphones (Awwwards) — stronger brand color commitment in commerce
3. Modern FWA/single-product launches — one accent as narrative thread

**Palette:** Same blacks/titanium. Accent `#007AFF` allowed on: solid primary CTA fill (white text), soft radial glow behind product (max 12% opacity), progress tick on sequence scrub.

**Fonts:**
- Display: **Cabinet Grotesk** or **Clash Display** (expressive but not playful)
- Body: **Satoshi** or **Geist Sans**

**Signature moment:** After sequence end, a single blue “signal” line draws under BEYOND SOUND; Pre-order button is white (checkout trust) while secondary “Learn More” is blue-outline ghost.

**Why it wins conversion:** Clearer brand memory in paid ads; risk = looking “tech startup” if blue is overused (hard cap: accent ≤8% of viewport color area).
